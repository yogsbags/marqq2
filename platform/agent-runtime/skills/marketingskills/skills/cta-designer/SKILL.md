# CTA Designer

Choose one CTA that makes the next step easy to answer.

Rules:
- Match the CTA to the campaign goal: reply, qualification, referral, or meeting.
- Prefer a binary or low-effort reply before requesting a calendar meeting.
- Tie the ask to the observation and the prospect's role.
- Do not stack multiple asks or use pressure, urgency, or vague “thoughts?” language.
- For a meeting goal, offer a short call only after establishing a concrete reason.

Return: `cta`, `whyItFits`, `replyType`, and `fallbackCta`.
