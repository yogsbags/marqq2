## 1. Project-basis archetypes that fit a senior hospital operator

| Archetype | Typical duration | What they pay | Who hires |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Interim CEO / MD** post PE close | 12–24 mo | ₹1.5–2.5 Cr/yr all-in (India) · $15–30K/mo (GCC) · plus success bonus on exit | PE funds that just acquired, founders in transition |
| **100-day value creation sprint** | 3–6 mo | $50–150K fixed + KPI bonus | PE fund pre-close or immediately post-close |
| **Integration COO** (post-merger) | 12–18 mo | ₹1.2–2 Cr/yr | Merging hospital chains (Aster, Manipal, Fortis patterns) |
| **Concession CEO** (KSA/UAE) | 3–5 yr contract | $250–500K/yr all-in + perf bonus + equity | Winning concession consortium, PIF Health Holding cluster SPVs |
| **O&M partner lead** (real-estate-led hospital) | 5–10 yr management contract | Mgmt fee 3-6% of revenue + perf + ESOP | Real estate developers (Arada, Brigade, Prestige, etc.) |
| **Turnaround CEO** (struggling hospital) | 12–24 mo | $25–50K/mo (GCC) · ₹15–25 lakh/mo (India) + turnaround bonus | PE funds, lenders, stressed-asset buyers |
| **Buy-side due diligence operator** | 4–8 weeks | $30–60K/engagement | PE funds pre-close |
| **Cluster / regional COO** | 18–36 mo | ₹80 lakh – 1.5 Cr/yr | Multi-hospital chains scaling |
| **Greenfield / de novo hospital project director** | 24–36 mo | ₹1–1.8 Cr/yr + ramp bonus | Real estate + operator consortia, GCC developers |
| **Pre-IPO professionalization lead** | 9–15 mo | ₹1–2 Cr/yr + ESOP | PE-backed chains heading to listing |

**Bench rules for project basis:**
- Day rate for senior hospital operator in India: **₹1–3 lakh/day** (US$1,200–3,600)
- Day rate for senior GCC operator: **$1,500–4,000/day**
- Monthly retainer (interim COO/CEO): India **₹8–25 lakh/mo**; GCC **$10–30K/mo**; US **$15–25K/mo**
- 100-day plan: **$50K–$200K fixed** depending on scope + carry / KPI bonus
- 3-month transformation: **$40K–$80K**
