## 5 · Tax & Structure — The Family-Office Optimization Layer

### Forest City SFO Wrapper (10+10 Year Tax Holiday)
- **AUM:** ≥RM30M (single tier) or ≥RM50M (multi-tier)
- **Local investment requirement:** RM10M or 10% of AUM (designated sectors count 1.5x)
- **Tax:** 0% on qualifying income (dividends, interest, capital gains from permitted investments)
- **Family count:** At least 4 family members
- **Physical office:** Required at Pulau 1, Forest City (≥450 sq ft)
- **Why this matters:** A family office that wraps founder deals in the SFO structure gets **0% effective tax on exits** — adds 4–8% to net IRR over 10+ years

### Angel Tax Incentive (ATI) for ECF
- 0% effective tax on investment up to RM500K/year per accredited investor
- Investor must be accredited by Malaysian Business Angel Network (MBAN)
- High-net-worth (RM3M+ net assets) or high income (RM180K+ individual / RM250K+ joint)
- **Why this matters:** For $1M ticket across 5 ECF founder deals, ATI saves ~$200K in tax — directly IRR-accretive

### MyCIF + CoSIF Matching (2026 Active)
- **MyCIF** (Budget 2019, ongoing): Up to RM6B deployed; co-invests on 1:1 to 4:1 ratio
- **CoSIF** (Budget 2026, new): RM200M Strategic Co-Investment Fund; matching for ECF + P2P; JS-SEZ priority
- **Why this matters:** Put in $1M, get up to $4M sovereign matching — effectively triples/quadruples ticket while sharing risk

### Capital Gains Tax (CGT) & Foreign-Source Income
- Malaysia introduced CGT in 2024 on unlisted shares (disposals after 1 Jan 2024)
- 10% on net gain (resident); 10% on gross (non-resident)
- SFO wrapper exempts from CGT on qualifying investments
- **Why this matters:** Founder secondary block purchases ARE subject to CGT — SFO structure saves 10% on exit
