## 1 · Executive Summary — Why Malaysia, Why Now

Malaysia is the **highest-conviction SEA mid-market destination in 2026** for >10% IRR deployment at $1M–$100M tickets. Three structural tailwinds line up:

1. **Johor–Singapore SEZ (JS-SEZ)** is fully operational. RM76.98B (US$17.4B) in approved investments, 57% in implementation. RM73B more in IMFC-J pipeline. 100 projects / 11 sectors / 10 years. Tax holidays for manufacturing, financial services, logistics, green economy, healthcare, education. *(The Straits Times, July 2026; Invest Johor / IRDA)*
2. **LSS6 solar RFP is LIVE** — 2.65 GW solar + 1.25 GW BESS being auctioned by Suruhanjaya Tenaga. RFP documents available 27 Jul – 28 Aug 2026. Direct-equity IRR **10–15%** (LSS) and **12–20%** (CRESS, corporate PPA). 21-year TNB offtake. *(Suruhanjaya Tenaga, July 2026; Trexon; RHB Research)*
3. **Healthcare consolidation** at a 12–22x EV/EBITDA inflection. IHH (Island Hospital, RM3.92B), pending TPG/Hong Leong/TE Asia merger target (CY26 listing, RM20B+ valuation), KPJ expansion to 5,000 beds by CY28. Implied peer arbitrage: 2–3x re-rating. *(MIDF, May 2025; KLSEScreener; MIDF Healthcare Thematic)*

**Net for an LP:** You can build a $50–100M Malaysia book at >10% net IRR today across renewable infra, mid-market PE co-invest, REITs, and IPO primary — most with sovereign or GLC co-investors de-risking the entry.
