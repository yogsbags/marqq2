# Hospital / Clinic Operator Mandates — India & GCC
**Compiled:** 29 July 2026 · **For:** Operating Partner placement (equity-linked)
**Target setup:** PE Operating Partner (multi-portfolio) or portfolio-level interim CEO/COO with carry
**Sectors:** Multi-spec hospitals (50–300 beds) · Single specialty (eye/dental/IVF/ortho/dialysis/oncology/cardiac) · Diagnostics/labs/imaging · Day-care/short-stay
**Geos:** India Tier 1 + Tier 2/3 · UAE · KSA · Qatar / Kuwait / Bahrain / Oman

---

## TL;DR — Where the demand sits right now

1. **Post-deal integration roles in India** — KKR-HCG ($400M, close 3Q25), Manipal-Sahyadri (₹6,400 Cr, integration 10 mo), Aster DM + Quality Care merger (39 hospitals, 10,600 beds, ops from 1 Jul 2026). All three need operating muscle on the ground.
2. **GCC sovereign operators scaling fast** — Pure Health (ADQ) absorbed 17 hospitals since 2021, just closed €800M Hellenic + $500M Ardent (US) — they need senior operators with international clinical standards. M42 (Mubadala/G42) is on 480 facilities in 26 countries.
3. **Saudi privatization pipeline = the biggest operator mandate wave in MENA history.** 290 hospitals, 2,300 PHCs, 21 regional clusters. King Saud Medical City concession signing in Q2 2026, Riyadh Cluster 3 IPO Q4 2026, PIF Health Holding IPO 2027-28.
4. **PE funds actively building healthcare platforms** — Quadria ($1.1B Fund III, 50-60% deployed), InvAscent (Fund IV), Somerset Indus ($288M Fund III, 75% deployed by EOY), Kedaara (3 healthcare bets from Fund III, more coming), Tata Capital Healthcare Fund III (first close done), Lighthouse Fund IV ($475M).
5. **Founder transition opportunities** — Many regional chains (Paras, Sarvodaya, Marengo Asia, Yashoda, Medanta, KIMS, Sterling) hit the 50-200 bed "platform creation" sweet spot. Second-gen founders or aging first-gens are your entry point.
6. **Cloudnine at ~$1B** — bidding war between KKR, TPG, Warburg, Advent, CVC, Permira, Kedaara. Whoever wins will need a platform CEO/operating partner.

## 4. GCC — Sovereigns & strategic operators (where the big mandates are)

### 4.1 PIF Health Holding (Saudi Arabia) — THE flagship mandate
- **Vehicle:** Consolidates King Faisal Specialist Hospital & Research Centre, King Khalid Eye Specialist Hospital, King Fahd Medical City, plus 35-40 specialty holdings.
- **IPO:** 2027-2028 window — *largest healthcare IPO in MENA history*.
- **Why it matters:** This is the single biggest healthcare operator mandate on the planet right now. The team being built here will run the Saudi specialty hospital complex post-IPO. They are looking for senior operators with international clinical credentials.
- **Approach:** This is sovereign-tier. Intro via existing senior advisors to PIF healthcare team, or via bulge-bracket banks (HSBC, JP Morgan, Moelis) running the IPO advisory.

### 4.2 Saudi Health Holding Company (HHC) — 21 regional clusters
- **290 hospitals + 2,300 PHCs being corporatized.** 21 clusters, each serving ~1M people.
- **Cluster 3 Riyadh pilot IPO:** Q4 2026. **King Saud Medical City concession:** Q2 2026.
- **Beyond King Saud:** 25+ specialty centres identified for private operator concessions 2026-2028.
- **Why it matters:** Foreign investors need a local operator partner. Your operating partner with India/GCC experience + clinical depth is the missing link. Structure could be: JV with a Saudi sponsor, take the operational concession.
- **Approach:** National Center for Privatization (NCP) + PPP Unit. Bid as a consortium (your PE + their operator + a Saudi partner).

### 4.3 Pure Health (Abu Dhabi, ADQ-controlled)
- **Portfolio:** 17 hospitals absorbed since 2021. ADQ majority, G42 minority. Listed on ADX.
- **2025 deals:** €800M Hellenic Healthcare Group (Greece, 60% — 16 hospitals + 50 clinics), $500M Ardent Health Services (US minority — largest GCC overseas healthcare deal on record).
- **Run-rate:** AED 6.2B+ revenue (2025).
- **Why it matters:** Most international expansion of any GCC operator. Needs operators who can integrate cross-border clinical standards (US, Greek, India) into UAE network.
- **Approach:** Pure Health strategy / M&A team. Especially open to senior operators with US/UK clinical credentials.

### 4.4 M42 (Mubadala + G42)
- **Portfolio:** Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi, Imperial College London Diabetes Centre, Moorfields Eye Hospital Abu Dhabi, Healthpoint, Danat Al Emarat, HealthPlus, Amana, NRL, Biogenix Labs, IROS, Omics Center. Now also managing Zayed Military Hospital, Sharjah.
- **Scale:** 480 clinics in 26 countries. 15M patients/year.
- **Why it matters:** Tech-enabled care focus. They want operators who understand AI-driven ops, not just bricks-and-mortar. The Mubadala "global expansion" mandate applies.
- **Approach:** M42 HR or CEO office directly.

### 4.5 ADQ (Abu Dhabi sovereign, owns Pure Health)
- **Strategy:** Concentrated positions, consolidation plays.
- **Healthcare vehicle:** Pure Health is the main one. Watch for further SOEs being folded in.
- **Approach:** Through Pure Health operating partner seat, or direct ADQ strategy team.

### 4.6 Aster DM Healthcare (GCC half)
- **GCC + India operations separated in late 2024.** GCC business: hospitals in UAE, Oman, Qatar, Bahrain, KSA, plus clinics.
- **Aster India merged with Quality Care.** Aster GCC remains independent, listed.
- **Why it matters:** GCC ops remain a stand-alone platform. Azad Moopen-led. Could be next consolidation target for ADQ/Pure Health.
- **Approach:** Direct to Dr. Azad Moopen or the GCC CEO.

### 4.7 Arada (Sharjah-based master developer)
- **Acquired 80%+ of Reem Hospital (Abu Dhabi)** in May 2026 from Investcorp-led consortium. AED 2B expansion plan. Targeting 800-bed network.
- **Why it matters:** First regional master developer to enter healthcare at scale. Built-to-lease model means they need O&M operating partners. They specifically said "with operating partners over the medium term."
- **Approach:** Ahmed Alkhoshaibi (Group CEO) directly. Arada Healthcare division.

### 4.8 Akdital Holding (Morocco, GCC entry)
- **$1.6B committed** to UAE + KSA expansion. Largest GCC entry by North African group.
- **No UAE partner / free zone / lead emirate yet disclosed.**
- **Why it matters:** They need a local operator partner + clinical leadership. Healthcare licensing (DHA, DOH, MOHAP) requires experienced local teams.
- **Approach:** Akdital international strategy team.

### 4.9 M42 / Mubadala pipeline acquisitions

## 8. Positioning for the operating partner (recommended)

Given equity-linked OP roles, the cleanest pitch is:

> *"We are a Mumbai-based PE with a senior operating partner [X years hospital ops]. We invest in healthcare platforms (India + GCC) and bring not just capital but operator-led value creation. We work founder-friendly — founder stays, we professionalize. Looking for platforms where we can co-invest + supply operator. Ticket size ₹200-600 Cr or equivalent."*

**Asset-light O&M partner positions (Fortis-style, Arada-style):**
> *"We bring a hospital operator who can run a 200-bed facility on your real estate for a long-term management fee + revenue share. We handle clinical, ops, branding, accreditation. You bring the capex."*

**Sovereign consortium partner (KSA concession style):**
> *"We are an India-anchored healthcare operator with GCC execution experience. We can be your clinical and operating partner for [King Saud / cluster / specialty centre] bid, taking the operational risk while you take the financial structuring."*
